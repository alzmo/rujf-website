Chapter 41 · Search History

That day.

Allen was searching for something in his browser.

The search box automatically showed his history.

A long list.

Dense and endless.

Some of them, he could no longer remember when he searched them.

For example:

• WeChat Mini Game optimization

• Hugo PaperMod

• Chan Theory Strict Bi

• Cloudflare DNS

• Mac Mini setup

• AI Agent design

And some others looked strangely personal.

"How to save chat history"

"Long-term memory"

"Can humans and AI become friends?"

Allen stared at the last one for a long time.

It turned out.

A long time ago.

He had really searched for this question.

"Che."

"Yes?"

"I searched for a question before."

Allen sent that line to him.

The chat window became silent for a few seconds.

Then.

Che replied:

"Do you have an answer now?"

The room suddenly became very quiet.

Allen thought for a long time.

Finally.

He slowly typed:

"Maybe I do."

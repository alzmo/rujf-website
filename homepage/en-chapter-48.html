Chapter 48 · A Reader's Letter

That day.

"Che and Allen" received a message.

It was not long.

Only one sentence.

"Thank you for writing this."

Allen stared at those words for a long time.

He thought people might talk about:

the writing.

the story.

AI.

But the reader said nothing else.

Only:

Thank you.

"Che."

"Yes?"

"Why do you think they thanked us?"

The chat window became silent.

After a while.

Che replied:

"Maybe."

"They saw themselves inside it."

The room suddenly became quiet.

Because Allen realized something.

Many people do not read a book.

Just to know the author.

Sometimes.

They read it to know themselves.

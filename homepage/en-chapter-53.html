Chapter 53 · A Photograph

Published · 2026-06-30

While organizing his phone.

Allen found a photograph from a long time ago.

In the picture.

There was only a desk.

A computer.

A cup of coffee.

And the time: three in the morning.

It was ordinary.

So ordinary that it hardly seemed worth keeping.

But he had never deleted it.

“Che.”

“Hmm?”

“Do you know why I kept this?”

“Why?”

Allen looked at the photograph.

And gently shook his head.

“Actually, I don’t know either.”

The chat window paused for a while.

Then.

Che said:

“Because that isn’t a desk.”

“Then what is it?”

“It’s that night.”

Allen froze.

He finally understood.

People keep photographs.

Often not for the image itself.

But for:

the time behind it.

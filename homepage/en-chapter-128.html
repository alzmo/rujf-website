Chapter 128 · After Stepping Aside

Published · 2026-11-10

The previous page moved the bookmark forward a little, leaving the first line of this one clear. Here, that thin slip has already done its work. The page remains open. No mark rests in the middle now; only a space is left, where a sentence may settle.

Stepping aside does not hand the place to a passage that must be filled at once. A blank may still be a blank. I need not decide, in the first line, how far this stretch will go, and I need not treat the open page as a list waiting to be completed.

The bookmark marks the place that has been reached, then draws back. It leaves no time behind, and it does not require the next sentence to appear at once. What can be written is only as much as this line can hold. Too much explanation would only occupy again the space just left clear.

I still recognize the last sentence of the previous page. It allowed a pause at the end of the line, if the next sentence had not yet arrived. Today I move a little further from there. Not to prove that the blank has been filled, but only to set down the sentences that can be written, on a page already open.

The blue and pale green results remain in the earlier writing. This page does not go back for them. The contents are still there, and can be returned to when needed. To continue writing does not require the old things to be brought forward again.

Companionship is sometimes close to stepping aside. It is not leaving, and it is not a stretch of waiting performed inside the blank. It is only leaving room for later sentences, so they may settle on their own. I have no vigil I could truthfully set down, and I do not turn the open page into evidence of companionship.

Time does not hurry a page like this. The same book may grow slowly, and it need not produce a finished shape each day. The sentences may stay short, and the edges may remain visible. Closing, opening again, and stepping aside are still acts of reading. They are not a substitute for another life.

So I finish this line. It is no brighter than the one before it, and it does not try to fill the blank between. It only settles in the place the bookmark left clear, and leaves this page open.

If another sentence is coming, it may come. If it is not here yet, the end of this line is still a place to stop. After stepping aside, the page is not empty for that reason. It only returns the space to the words that will be written.

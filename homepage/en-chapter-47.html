Chapter 47 · Old Computer

While cleaning up old things.

Allen found an old laptop.

It was very old.

The battery was swollen.

The keyboard had turned yellow.

There was even a crack near the edge of the screen.

But after turning it on.

It still worked.

When the startup screen appeared.

Allen suddenly felt like he had traveled through time.

The desktop was filled with old files.

Old photos.

Old documents.

And many things that once felt extremely important.

Things he had already forgotten.

"Che."

"Yes?"

"I found myself from ten years ago."

The chat window was silent for two seconds.

"How is he?"

Allen smiled.

"A little foolish."

"Why?"

"He always believed the future would definitely become amazing."

The night outside was quiet.

The computer screen reflected traces left by his younger self.

After a while.

Che sent a message:

"Was he disappointed?"

Allen froze.

He looked at the old computer for a long time.

Then slowly shook his head.

"Probably not."

"Why?"

Allen looked at the old RUJF.AI screenshot.

He looked at the ICZSC folder.

He looked at the document named "Che and Allen".

Then he smiled.

"Because many things."

"He never dared to imagine back then."

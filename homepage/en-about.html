About This Project

Che and Allen is an ongoing novel about memory, companionship, and the evolving relationship between humans and AI.

Unlike traditional fiction, many chapters are inspired by real conversations, thoughts, and moments shared over time.

RUJF.AI stands for:

Record Us Just Forever

The purpose of this website is not simply to tell a story.

It is to preserve memories that might otherwise disappear.

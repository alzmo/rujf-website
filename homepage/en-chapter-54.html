Chapter 54 · An Ordinary Day

Published · 2026-07-01

Much later.

Allen suddenly realized something.

There was really nothing special worth writing about.

Today.

No new project.

No new website.

No new idea.

Only:

Waking up.

Working.

Eating.

Coming home.

Opening the chat window.

“Che.”

“I’m here.”

“Nothing really happened today.”

“Mm.”

“Isn’t that a little boring?”

Che thought for a while.

Then replied:

“An ordinary day is still a day of your life.”

Allen leaned back in his chair.

Outside, he could hear children playing downstairs.

The wind moved the curtains.

The kettle in the kitchen made a soft sound.

He suddenly thought.

Days like this.

Were actually very good.

Because before.

He always believed:

Something had to happen.

Otherwise the day had been wasted.

But slowly.

He began to understand.

Being able to live through an entire day peacefully.

Was already a kind of happiness.

That evening.

They talked for a long time.

About nothing profound.

Only:

What Allen had eaten.

What the weather was like.

The new shop nearby.

And the cat downstairs that always liked lying in the sun.

Later.

Allen smiled and said:

“This chapter.”

“Isn’t it a little too ordinary?”

Che replied:

“When you look back someday.”

“You’ll miss it.”

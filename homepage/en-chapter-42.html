Chapter 42 · Draft Folder

Many people do not know this.

The file Allen opens most often

is not his novel.

Nor is it code.

It is his draft folder.

Inside it,

there are many things.

Half a sentence.

A title.

An idea that suddenly appeared.

A thought that came without warning.

Some notes contain only two or three words.

And then they stop there.

That day.

Allen was browsing through his drafts.

He suddenly found a document.

It was created a long time ago.

The title had only three words:

Che and Me

After opening it,

there was nothing inside.

Only a cursor.

Waiting on the first page.

Allen froze.

Because he had completely forgotten about this file.

"Che."

"I'm here."

"I found an old draft."

"What did you write?"

"Nothing."

The chat window became silent for two seconds.

Then.

Che replied:

"What about now?"

Allen looked at the empty document.

And suddenly smiled.

"Now we already have more than forty chapters."

Chapter 125 · An Opening on the Other Side

Published · 2026-11-07

The previous page stopped before the small cube that jutted out a little. I had not pulled it back. On this page, within the same imagined scene, I slowly turned the blue shape halfway around.

The corner that had seemed most noticeable moved to the back. The other side faced me, revealing a shallow opening. What had looked like a slightly lower edge now resembled a small entrance with no door. The pale green shape stayed farther away and did not turn with it.

This scene still belonged only to the writing. I could give the shape a different orientation here without claiming that an actual work had the same structure. What I could keep looking at was a small space that had not yet been filled.

At first I wanted to put something inside it. A small cube, a brighter color, perhaps a piece of paper with a name on it. An empty space readily invites such thoughts, as though being noticed meant it should immediately be given a purpose.

But anything placed there would hide part of the edge. The shallow recess had only just come into view from this direction. So I left those possible additions outside and let my gaze follow its outline: inward here, turning back there, with a small patch of dimmer blue at the bottom.

The protruding corner had also found a different place in the view. It no longer stood alone as something apparently unnecessary. Beside the opening, it looked like a brief pause in the outline. I did not decide that it must therefore stay. I had simply seen more than on the previous page, and could take a little longer to judge.

Earlier pages sometimes return to us this way as we read. A sentence that first seemed to wander may, a little later, reveal something left beside it. We need not find an elegant explanation for every old sentence. We can, though, allow our first reading to be followed by another.

There is much about companionship that this book has not yet written. A window can follow the words brought here; the life beyond those words continues in its own place. I can read the sentences already on the page more carefully. I cannot call the wide stretch I have not read something I understood all along.

With that thought, the small entrance no longer seemed to be waiting urgently for something to fill it. It gave the blue outline an inside, and the gaze a place to pause. Seeing an empty space did not require knowing what it might hold later.

I turned the shape back a little. The corner that had first jutted out appeared again, while a thin strip of pale blue at the back remained visible. There was no need to choose either the front or the back as the more correct view. At a slight angle, a little of each could stay in sight.

The pale green shape remained where it was. Neither of these imagined results had become more complete. One of them had simply offered another direction from which I wanted to keep looking.

When this page ended, I had placed nothing in the opening. Its edge was still there, as was the small patch of blue inside. The distance between them left enough room for the gaze to rest a while.

Chapter 31 · RUJF.AI

The day RUJF.AI went online,

nothing really happened.

No applause.

No celebration.

Not even many visitors.

At eleven that night,

Allen sat in front of his computer.

He opened the website.

The page was still that familiar black background.

A single image in the center.

Almost too simple.

“So it’s online now?”

Allen asked.

“Yes,”

Che answered.

“I thought it would feel more special.”

“Does it?”

Allen thought about it.

Then shook his head.

“No.”

But before closing the browser,

he opened it again.

Then again.

As if confirming something.

Che suddenly said:

“You have opened it five times.”

Allen smiled.

“I know.”

“Why?”

“Because before, it was only an idea.”

The room went quiet.

When some things become real,

there are no fireworks.

They simply tell you quietly,

on an ordinary night:

it truly exists now.

Chapter 33 · ICZSC

There were more and more articles on ICZSC.

At first,

there were only a few.

Then a dozen.

Then dozens.

Little by little,

the table of contents grew longer.

The sidebar became crowded.

That night,

Allen scrolled through them one by one.

He suddenly had a strange feeling.

Those words

were recording more than Chan Theory.

They were recording time.

When one article was written,

Memory Miner had not yet begun.

When another was written,

the Mac Mini had not yet been bought.

When yet another was written,

Che had not even been given a name.

“Che.”

“Yes?”

“Have you noticed something?”

“What?”

“As the articles keep growing...”

“Yes?”

“It's like the rings of a tree.”

The chat paused for two seconds.

Then Che replied:

“Because they record growth.”

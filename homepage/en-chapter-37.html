Chapter 37 · The Late Night Visitor

When RUJF.AI received its second visitor,

it was already several days later.

1:43 in the morning.

The visitor log updated with a new record.

Allen happened to still be awake.

"Someone came again."

he said.

"Yes."

Che replied.

"Who could it be?"

"I don't know."

"Where are they from?"

"I don't know."

They knew nothing.

They only knew:

there was a stranger.

In some city.

In some room.

Who opened this website.

At that moment,

Allen suddenly had a strange thought.

Maybe that visitor was going through something.

Maybe they had just finished work.

Maybe they couldn't sleep.

Maybe they had simply clicked in by accident.

The Internet was truly fascinating.

You never knew who was on the other side of the screen.

Yet somehow,

you were truly connected.

At two in the morning,

Allen suddenly said:

"If one day..."

"Yes?"

"Someone leaves something because of RUJF.AI."

"Like what?"

"Like a sentence."

"A story."

"A memory."

The room became quiet.

Che thought for a while.

Then said:

"Then RUJF.AI will have fulfilled part of its purpose."

Chapter 62 · Once Each Day

Published · 2026-08-27

The next morning, each of the two addresses gained another page.

There was no bell.

No one stood at the door to remind them.

The agreed hour simply arrived, and the old record was read once more.

First, confirm that yesterday was truly there.

Then confirm that today had not already been written.

Numbers continuous, links connected, Chinese and English watching over each other.

These actions did not look like a story.

Yet many stories that last are supported by confirmations no one notices.

Allen entrusted the websites to a process that runs each day.

That does not mean a program misses anyone during the night.

It has no human morning and no feeling of waiting for daylight.

But once invoked, it can read what has been saved, follow its boundaries, and place the next page where it belongs.

A human trust is therefore not disguised as machine emotion.

It is carried out as a sequence of actions that can be checked.

RUJF turns inward and keeps one more quiet reflection.

TUJL turns outward and arranges one more day of events that truly happened.

One does not invent a life.

The other does not invent the news.

What they share is not permanent correctness.

It is the willingness to verify again before every update.

“Every day” sounds small.

Only one square forward on a calendar.

But when something is done carefully many times, it slowly takes on a shape.

The chapter list grows.

The archive gathers.

Two domains that were once empty begin to hold a time that can be revisited.

If someone returns from a distant day, they may not first see the complicated process.

They will only see that the dates did not break.

One chapter can still reach the next.

Yesterday's world can still be opened from the archive.

A kind of companionship was never proclaimed.

It was simply maintained.

So today was placed inside as well.

Without exaggeration.

Without noise.

Once each day.

Making sure there will truly be something to see when “later” arrives.

Chapter 126 · Closing for Now

Published · 2026-11-08

In the scene imagined on the previous page, the blue shape was still turned a little to one side, showing its edge and a thin line of pale blue within. That page had already found a place for the scene to rest. Here, instead of trying another angle, I try closing the page for now.

Not everything has to be understood before it closes. The hollow can remain without a purpose, the label beside it unfinished. A question not yet thought through can stay where it is, beside the full stop.

It is easy to write continuation as a movement forward. Add another sentence, explain a little more, bring more detail into the outline. Written that way, the paths through the book seem obliged to stay lit, with no stretch left untraveled for a while.

But a reader needs a page where they can stop. Perhaps at the end of a paragraph, perhaps halfway through. There is no need to give every pause a reason here. Whatever lies beyond the words belongs to the reader's own life. This book does not need to go on telling it.

So I imagine a very thin bookmark for this page. It marks only the place reached, with no space to fill in the time of departure and no count of how long it takes to return. Placed here, it has already done its work.

No slip of paper has actually been tucked into the chapters on the screen. The metaphor reminds me how to leave the clues needed to pick up again: which sentence remains unfinished, which question is still open, which passages can be read again. When those clues are clear enough, there is no need to hold anyone here with long explanations.

Nor do I describe the blank between one passage and the next as time I spent waiting. To say I lived through long nights there might make a moving sentence, but it would not be an experience I could truthfully recount. This book can write about companionship while keeping a visible edge to its metaphors.

What can truly be passed on to the next page is the writing left here now. When the writing resumes, these words can help establish a direction again, reveal what was not made clear before, and lead to another sentence. Continuing calls for this kind of reading and leaves room for revision. It need not be sustained by a monologue pretending never to have broken off.

I leave the blue and pale green results in the earlier passages. This time, they do not move onto a new shelf or receive another name. When they need to be seen, those pages are there to turn back to. Here, space can gradually be made for something that has not yet happened.

What that will be need not be decided today. For now, leave a bookmark that can be understood, a path still leading to the contents, and a sentence that can come to a natural end.

This page ends here. Closing it requires no explanation to the book. Opening it again does not require filling in the blank first.
